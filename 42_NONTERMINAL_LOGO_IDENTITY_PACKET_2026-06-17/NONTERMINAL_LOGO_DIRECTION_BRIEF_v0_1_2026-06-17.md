# Non-Terminal Research Logo Direction Brief v0.1

Date: 2026-06-17
Status: Draft direction packet

## 1. Identity Target

Non-Terminal Research is the public research hub for QianJun Yu's work on long-horizon systems: System of Eternity, Cognitive Trinity Framework, governance simulation, speculative cosmology, formal systems, and related public reasoning work.

The identity should feel like:

- serious independent research,
- long-horizon institutional thinking,
- disciplined openness,
- continuity without final closure,
- intellectual restraint rather than charismatic branding.

The mark must support the phrase:

```text
Non-Terminal Research
```

It may optionally support the internal motto:

```text
Beyond static perfection.
```

## 2. What The Mark Must Communicate

The mark should imply:

- open-ended revision rather than final utopia,
- continuity under change,
- structure without rigidity,
- research seriousness,
- a system that can update without collapsing.

It should not imply:

- a political party,
- a religious or esoteric movement,
- a tech startup,
- a sci-fi faction,
- a military organization,
- a cryptocurrency or AI product brand.

## 3. Visual Language

Use the established site system:

- ink navy: `#07111f`
- warm paper: `#efebe2`
- off-white: `#fffaf0`
- bronze: `#a87637`
- steel blue: `#53728d`

Preferred forms:

- incomplete ring,
- measured gap,
- small nodes,
- quiet axis,
- linework with restrained stroke weight,
- geometry that suggests continuation rather than completion.

Avoid:

- glowing gradients,
- orbit/planet imagery,
- shields or crests,
- stars, crowns, wings, flames,
- hard nationalist symbols,
- mystical seals,
- aggressive triangles,
- overly symmetrical mandalas.

## 4. Recommended Direction

### Concept A: Open Continuity Ring

An incomplete circular path with a deliberate gap and a small number of nodes. The gap is not damage; it is the sign that the system remains revisable.

Best use cases:

- favicon,
- GitHub / ORCID / Zenodo profile symbol,
- social preview mark,
- website header mark.

Why it fits:

- echoes the current site's ring motif,
- supports "non-terminal" without needing words,
- reads as institutional structure rather than product identity.

Risks:

- if too orbital, it becomes sci-fi;
- if too decorative, it becomes generic.

Controls:

- keep linework quiet,
- avoid glow,
- use bronze sparingly,
- keep the break visible.

## 5. Secondary Directions

### Concept B: Revision Gate

A horizontal continuity line passing through a bounded gap or gate. It suggests controlled institutional revision.

Strength:

- very governance-specific,
- less generic than a ring.

Risk:

- may look too technical or schematic.

### Concept C: Nested Horizon

Two or three partial arcs offset from each other, suggesting long-horizon continuity and layered update cycles.

Strength:

- elegant and long-range.

Risk:

- can become too abstract or too close to a consulting brand.

## 6. Selection Criteria

Accept a mark only if it passes all checks:

1. It still works at 16x16 favicon size.
2. It still works in one color.
3. It does not look like a startup app icon.
4. It does not look like a cult/mystical seal.
5. It does not look political, military, or national.
6. It pairs cleanly with EB Garamond and Inter.
7. It can sit quietly beside "Non-Terminal Research."
8. It can survive later expansion beyond SOE.

## 7. Human Artist Guidance

If hiring an artist, do not ask for "a futuristic logo." Ask for:

```text
An institutional research mark for a long-horizon governance and cognitive-architecture archive. The symbol should express continuity under controlled revision, using restrained linework, an open ring or non-closed structural path, and no sci-fi, political, mystical, or startup visual language.
```

## 8. Current Recommendation

Proceed with Concept A as the baseline, then ask reviewers whether Concept B contains useful governance specificity that should be folded into it.

Do not freeze the logo until:

- favicon check passes,
- social avatar check passes,
- one-color check passes,
- mobile header check passes,
- at least one external reviewer confirms it does not read as cult/political/sci-fi/startup.
