# Review Prompt: Non-Terminal Research Logo Direction

Use this prompt for AI reviewers or a human design reviewer.

## Context

Non-Terminal Research is the public research hub of QianJun Yu. It houses work on the System of Eternity, Cognitive Trinity Framework, governance simulation, speculative cosmology, formal systems, and related long-horizon research.

The site visual direction is already accepted:

- dark ink-navy header,
- warm paper body,
- bronze and steel accents,
- EB Garamond headings,
- Inter body text,
- restrained institutional tone.

The logo should not become louder than the research.

## Materials To Review

1. `NONTERMINAL_LOGO_DIRECTION_BRIEF_v0_1_2026-06-17.md`
2. `logo_concepts_gallery.html`
3. SVG concept files in `concepts/`

## Review Questions

1. Which concept is strongest for a serious independent research hub?
2. Does any concept look like a tech startup, political movement, cult/mystical symbol, sci-fi faction, crypto project, or generic consulting brand?
3. Does the mark communicate non-terminal continuity, revision, and institutional seriousness?
4. Which concept is most likely to work as a favicon and social avatar?
5. Which concept should be discarded?
6. What 3 to 5 changes would improve the strongest concept before a human artist or final implementation?

## Verdict Options

Choose exactly one:

```text
ACCEPT_LOGO_DIRECTION_CONCEPT_A
ACCEPT_LOGO_DIRECTION_CONCEPT_B
ACCEPT_LOGO_DIRECTION_CONCEPT_C
PATCH_NEEDED_LOGO_DIRECTION
REDESIGN_LOGO_DIRECTION
```

## Boundary

This review is only for visual identity direction. It does not authorize claims about SOE readiness, institutional endorsement, deployment, empirical validation, or public-policy authority.
