# SOE Logo Patch Directions

Date: 2026-06-18

Purpose: test corrected O treatments after multi-AI review flagged the four-cardinal-gap O as potentially reading as a reticle, loading spinner, or technical UI mark.

These are not final logos. They are narrow geometry tests for the O only.

## Current Recommendation

Use Variant 1 as the lead direction:

`variant-1-single-gap-open-ring.svg`

Reason: it preserves the non-terminal/open-continuity idea while removing the crosshair structure created by four symmetric gaps.

## Variants

1. `variant-1-single-gap-open-ring.svg`
   - One intentional upper-right opening.
   - Lowest reticle risk.
   - Best for institutional use.

2. `variant-2-two-gap-offset-ring.svg`
   - Two non-cardinal breaks.
   - More dynamic, but slightly less calm.
   - Useful if Variant 1 feels too plain.

3. `variant-3-solid-o-with-notch.svg`
   - Keeps O mostly typographic.
   - Lowest spinner risk.
   - May be too subtle as a non-terminal signal.

## Patch Principle

Do not add nodes, arrows, grids, crosshairs, stars, globes, pillars, circuitry, crypto geometry, or political/military insignia cues.

Keep the mark wordmark-first, typographic, archival, and restrained.
