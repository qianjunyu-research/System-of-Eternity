# Human Artist Brief: Non-Terminal Research Logo

## Project

Create a restrained logo / symbol system for **Non-Terminal Research**, the public research hub of QianJun Yu.

The mark should support serious theoretical research in:

- civilizational governance,
- cognitive architecture,
- governance simulation,
- long-horizon institutional design,
- formal systems.

## Core Idea

Non-terminal means: not a final utopia, not a fixed endpoint, not a closed ideology. It means a system that preserves continuity while retaining the capacity to revise itself.

The symbol should communicate:

```text
continuity under controlled revision
```

## Preferred Direction

Use an open or incomplete structural form:

- incomplete ring,
- measured gap,
- quiet nodes,
- subtle axis,
- non-closed path.

The gap should feel intentional, not broken.

## Visual Tone

Institutional, serious, calm, research-oriented.

Avoid:

- sci-fi,
- startup,
- crypto,
- political campaign,
- national crest,
- mystical seal,
- religious/cult-like emblem,
- glowing gradients,
- heroic mascots.

## Colors

Primary colors:

- ink navy: `#07111f`
- warm paper: `#efebe2`
- off-white: `#fffaf0`
- bronze: `#a87637`
- steel blue: `#53728d`

The mark must also work in one color.

## Deliverables

Minimum deliverables:

1. Primary horizontal lockup: symbol + "Non-Terminal Research"
2. Symbol-only mark
3. Favicon / small-size variant
4. Social avatar variant
5. One-color version
6. SVG and PNG exports

Optional:

1. Social banner treatment
2. Zenodo/GitHub/ORCID profile image
3. Simple usage guide

## Evaluation

The final mark must pass:

- readable at 16x16 favicon size,
- serious on an academic page,
- not overdesigned,
- not charismatic or ideological,
- not dependent on animation,
- compatible with EB Garamond and Inter typography.
