# SOE Hostile Sprint RCC-01 Design vs Execution Clarification

Status: RCC-01 BOUNDARY CLARIFICATION / NO PARTICIPANT CONTACT AUTHORIZED

Date: 2026-06-22

## 1. Definition

RCC-01 means:

`Reality-Contact Checkpoint 01`

It does not mean Rapid Context Change, Review Coordination Center, or any other unrelated acronym expansion.

## 2. Design Completion vs Execution

RCC-01 has two different states:

| State | Meaning | Current authorization |
|---|---|---|
| RCC-01 design | Draft the source materials, participant profiles, questions, consent/privacy/data/withdrawal rules, failure states, and owner responsibilities. | Allowed as the next planning artifact. |
| RCC-01 execution | Recruit or contact participants, send materials to outside readers, collect responses, or interpret live participant feedback. | Not authorized. |

## 3. Placement

Folder 48 correctly makes RCC-01 design an entry gate before Wave 0 in any later execution plan.

This means:

- Wave 0 cannot begin until RCC-01 design exists or the packet records a hold.
- RCC-01 design does not itself recruit participants.
- RCC-01 execution requires separate written authorization.

## 4. Minimum RCC-01 Design Elements

A future RCC-01 design packet must include:

- participant profile A: low-context outside reader
- participant profile B: high-context adversarial domain reviewer
- source packet short enough for actual human review
- questions testing whether SOE is misread as deployment-ready, ideological, empirically validated, or authority-claiming
- consent statement
- privacy and data-minimization statement
- withdrawal or stop process
- no-pressure contact language
- failure consequences tied to exit states
- owner roles and target dates

## 5. Failure Consequence Rule

An RCC-01 failure cannot be reinterpreted into success after the fact.

If outside readers interpret SOE as deployment-ready, ideology, empirical validation, policy authority, or real-world proof, the required consequence is a hold, patch, redesign, or stop state.

## 6. Boundary

This clarification does not authorize any external contact, participant recruitment, survey, interview, tabletop, deployment, pilot, empirical validation, or Operation Layer freeze.
