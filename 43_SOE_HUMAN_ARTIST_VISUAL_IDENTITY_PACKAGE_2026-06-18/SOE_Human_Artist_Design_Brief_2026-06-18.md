# SOE Human Artist Design Brief

## Project

Design a visual identity system for:

```text
System of Eternity
SOE
```

SOE is a civilizational governance research framework under the broader public research hub:

```text
Non-Terminal Research
https://nonterminal.org/
```

SOE is not a political party, not a tech product, not a utopian ideology, and not an implementation-ready governance system. It is a theoretical governance architecture for long-horizon institutional continuity, adjudication, source boundaries, controlled revision, and non-terminal self-correction.

## Design Goal

The identity should make SOE feel:

- serious,
- institutional,
- restrained,
- durable,
- research-oriented,
- long-horizon,
- structurally intelligent,
- open to revision without looking unstable.

The mark should feel like it belongs on:

- a working paper,
- an academic research website,
- a long-term institutional framework,
- a governance architecture document,
- an archive or standards-like research body.

## Core Meaning

SOE is built around this idea:

```text
continuity without final closure
```

The identity may lightly suggest:

- revision without collapse,
- structure without rigidity,
- continuity under change,
- governance that can update itself,
- non-terminal institutional design.

Do not over-literalize this. The logo does not need to explain SOE by itself. Many strong marks do not carry heavy explicit meaning; they become meaningful through repeated use, quality, and association. SOE needs a mark that feels right before it tries to say too much.

## Existing Visual System To Respect

The current Non-Terminal Research site uses:

- ink navy: `#07111f`
- secondary ink: `#111d2d`
- warm paper: `#efebe2`
- paper cool: `#f7f5ee`
- off-white: `#fffaf0`
- bronze accent: `#a87637`
- steel blue: `#53728d`

Typography direction:

- editorial serif headings similar to EB Garamond,
- clean humanist sans body text similar to Inter,
- no futuristic display fonts,
- no startup-style rounded wordmark.

The final logo must work in monochrome first. Color is secondary.

## Important Correction From Prior Exploration

Earlier explorations used a broken circular O. This created problems:

- it looked like a circle with a bite taken out,
- it sometimes read as `SCE` instead of `SOE`,
- it risked looking like a loading spinner,
- it risked a reticle or target association,
- it did not feel beautiful enough to lock.

Therefore:

```text
Do not assume the final SOE mark must be a broken circle.
```

A circle, open ring, notch, seam, gate, line, interval, threshold, or typographic intervention may be explored, but the artist should choose the best visual solution.

## Preferred Strategy

Start from a wordmark-first identity system.

Priority order:

1. Make `SOE` read clearly and beautifully.
2. Make `System of Eternity` feel serious and publishable.
3. Add non-terminal meaning only if it improves the mark.
4. Prove the mark works at small size.
5. Prove the mark works in black and white.

Readable first. Symbolic second.

Aesthetic quality first. Conceptual cleverness last.

## Suggested Directions To Explore

These are not mandatory:

- refined SOE wordmark with subtle non-closed typographic detail,
- editorial wordmark with one controlled interruption or threshold,
- linear revision-gate motif instead of a circle,
- quiet document seal that does not look governmental or mystical,
- typographic ligature or spacing system around SOE,
- abstract structural mark paired with the full name.
- no overt symbol at all, if the wordmark is strong enough.

## Must Avoid

Avoid anything that reads as:

- tech startup,
- crypto token,
- app icon,
- loading spinner,
- reticle,
- military badge,
- political party mark,
- cult or mystical seal,
- sci-fi faction,
- national crest,
- glowing orbital/cosmic imagery,
- generic consulting logo,
- overdesigned AI-generated geometry.

## Success Test

The final mark should make a serious reader think:

```text
This is a disciplined research framework.
```

It should not make them think:

```text
This is a movement, product, game faction, ideology, or fantasy world.
```
