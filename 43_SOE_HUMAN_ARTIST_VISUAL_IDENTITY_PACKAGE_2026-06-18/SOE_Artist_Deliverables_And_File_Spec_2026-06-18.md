# SOE Artist Deliverables And File Spec

## Required Design Deliverables

Ask the artist for at least three distinct logo directions:

1. Wordmark-first SOE direction
2. Symbol-plus-wordmark direction
3. Conservative typographic fallback direction

Each direction should include:

- full lockup: `System of Eternity`
- short lockup: `SOE`
- horizontal version
- stacked version
- symbol-only or favicon version if a symbol exists
- dark background version
- light background version
- monochrome black version
- monochrome white version

## Required Export Files

For the final selected direction:

- master vector file: `.ai`, `.svg`, or `.pdf`
- clean SVG exports
- transparent PNG exports
- square avatar PNG: 512x512
- favicon PNG: 16x16, 32x32, 64x64
- social preview PNG: 1200x630
- paper-cover/header mark
- usage guide PDF or short notes file

## Commission Mechanics To Confirm Before Work Begins

Before commissioning the artist, confirm these practical terms in writing:

- estimated price or price range,
- payment schedule,
- expected start date and delivery date,
- number of included revision rounds,
- cost or process for extra revision rounds,
- whether interim concept thumbnails are included,
- whether final source files are included,
- whether the final identity is exclusive to QianJun Yu / SOE,
- whether the work is transferred as work-for-hire or licensed for broad public research use.

The recommended concept process is:

1. Initial monochrome exploration: 6 to 9 thumbnail directions.
2. Select 1 to 2 directions for refinement.
3. Finalize one direction only after small-size tests, monochrome tests, and rights checks pass.

## Small-Size Tests

The mark must be tested at:

- 16 px favicon
- 32 px favicon
- 48 px profile icon
- 64 px profile icon
- 128 px avatar

At those sizes:

- `SOE` should still read as SOE,
- no detail should collapse into a spinner,
- no detail should look like damage,
- no detail should resemble a target or reticle.

## Color Tests

Test on:

- warm paper background: `#efebe2`
- ink navy background: `#07111f`
- pure white
- pure black

The mark must survive in one color.

## Website Fit Test

The mark should feel compatible with:

- EB Garamond-like headings,
- Inter-like body text,
- warm paper page background,
- ink navy hero/header,
- bronze accent rules.

It does not need to copy the current website motif.

## Ownership And Rights

Before using the final logo publicly, confirm:

- QianJun Yu receives permission to use the logo for public research identity,
- commercial or public-web use is allowed,
- modification rights are clear,
- source/vector files are included,
- fonts used are listed and licensed for commercial and public research use,
- no stock-logo template or trademark-conflicting mark is used.
- the artist has checked for obvious visual confusion with existing institutions, universities, public agencies, research organizations, standards bodies, political marks, military marks, or commercial brands.

This is important because the logo may be used on public records, websites, profiles, and publications.

## Usage Sheet Requirement

Ask the artist for a one-page usage sheet that includes:

- the preferred primary lockup,
- acceptable light/dark variants,
- minimum size guidance,
- safe-zone rule, preferably expressed numerically,
- crop guidance for square avatars, wide banners, and paper covers,
- a short do/do-not list for future use.
