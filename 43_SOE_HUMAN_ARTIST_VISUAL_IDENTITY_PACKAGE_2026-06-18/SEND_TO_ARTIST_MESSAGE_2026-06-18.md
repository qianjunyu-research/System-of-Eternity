# Send To Artist Message

Hello,

I am looking for a professional logo / visual identity direction for the System of Eternity (SOE), a serious civilizational governance research framework under my public research hub, Non-Terminal Research.

Please see the attached brief for the full design context, deliverable list, rejected prior directions, file specifications, and review criteria. This message is only the short introduction.

The identity should feel institutional, restrained, academic, and long-horizon. It should not look like a tech startup, political movement, military badge, crypto project, sci-fi faction, cult symbol, or fantasy emblem.

The core idea behind the research is:

```text
continuity without final closure
```

SOE is about institutional structure that can preserve continuity while remaining able to revise itself. The logo may lightly suggest openness, revision, thresholds, continuity, or non-terminal structure, but it should not try to explain the whole theory. Prior explorations with broken circular O marks looked too much like a bitten circle, loading spinner, or target, so please do not treat the circle idea as mandatory.

The most important requirement is that the mark looks good, reads clearly, is memorable, and feels serious enough for public research papers, a website, Zenodo/arXiv records, and profile images.

Please explore at least three directions:

1. A wordmark-first SOE direction
2. A symbol-plus-wordmark direction
3. A conservative typographic fallback direction

The final identity should include:

- `SOE` short lockup
- `System of Eternity` full lockup
- dark and light versions
- monochrome versions
- favicon/avatar versions
- vector source files and SVG/PNG exports

Before work begins, please confirm your estimated price or price range, expected timeline, number of included revision rounds, source-file delivery, font licensing, and whether the final identity can be used publicly for research websites, papers, archives, and social profiles.

The current website style uses ink navy, warm paper, restrained bronze accents, editorial serif headings, and clean sans-serif body text. The logo should harmonize with that world but does not need to copy it.

Thank you.

QianJun Yu
