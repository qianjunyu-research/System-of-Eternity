## Purpose

This folder prepares the System of Eternity visual identity for a human artist or professional logo designer.

This version incorporates the multi-AI review patch requiring practical commission mechanics, rights/font licensing checks, trademark-confusion screening, 16 px favicon consistency, and attachment-reference language.

The previous AI-assisted logo explorations were useful for discovering what not to do, but they should not be treated as final art direction. The current priority is not to force a broken-ring circle mark. The priority is to give a human artist a clear, high-quality design problem:

```text
Create a serious, institutional, memorable identity system for SOE without making it look like a tech startup, cult, political movement, crypto project, military unit, or sci-fi faction.
```
