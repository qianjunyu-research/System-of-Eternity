# SOE Visual Identity Do / Do Not Guide

## Do

- Use restrained geometry.
- Use strong typography.
- Preserve clear reading of `SOE`.
- Prefer memorability over explanation.
- Let meaning be light and associative.
- Prefer editorial and institutional visual weight.
- Use warm paper and ink-like contrast.
- Make the mark work without color.
- Let the full name carry seriousness:

```text
System of Eternity
```

- Treat the mark as a research identity, not a campaign identity.
- Leave room for future expansion beyond current papers.

## Do Not

- Do not force a broken circle.
- Do not make the O look bitten, damaged, or chewed away.
- Do not make `SOE` read as `SCE`.
- Do not use four equal circular gaps.
- Do not use a centered dot if it creates target or coordinate associations.
- Do not animate the mark.
- Do not add stars, globes, planets, wings, shields, crowns, flames, triangles, or sacred geometry.
- Do not use neon, gradients, chrome, glow, glass, or "AI product" styling.
- Do not use red/black political force styling.
- Do not use blue corporate SaaS styling.
- Do not make the logo carry the whole theory.
- Do not accept a clever concept if the mark is ugly.

## Design Philosophy

The SOE identity should be quiet enough to survive criticism and strong enough to be remembered.

It should be:

- less charismatic than a movement,
- less decorative than a brand campaign,
- less futuristic than sci-fi,
- less literal than an infographic,
- more disciplined than a generic personal logo.

It does not need to be a diagram of SOE. It should be a usable identity that can gather meaning over time.

## Relationship To Non-Terminal Research

Non-Terminal Research is the broader public hub.

SOE is the main governance research branch.

The SOE mark may eventually be used for:

- Zenodo records,
- arXiv PDFs,
- GitHub/ORCID/social profile images,
- paper covers,
- website section headers,
- future presentation decks.

The SOE mark should harmonize with the website identity but does not need to copy the website motif.
