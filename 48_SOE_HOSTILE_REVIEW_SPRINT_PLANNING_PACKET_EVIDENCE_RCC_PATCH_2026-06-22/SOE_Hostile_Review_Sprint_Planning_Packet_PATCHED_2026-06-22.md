# SOE Hostile Review Sprint Planning Packet - Patched

Date: 2026-06-22
Status: EVIDENCE/RCC PATCHED PLANNING PACKET / NO EXECUTION AUTHORIZED

## 1. Purpose

This packet defines a hostile review sprint plan for the System of Eternity (SOE). The target is not to polish the framework. The target is to break it, identify where it overclaims, and specify what must be redesigned, patched, held, or downgraded before any later real-world test design is discussed.

The sprint assumes adversaries may include malicious coalitions, opportunistic insiders, propaganda actors, bribery or blackmail networks, hostile legal interpreters, institutional incumbents, economic oligarchy, ideological capture, state-aligned actors, and public panic cascades.

This packet is planning only. It does not run the sprint.

Evidence/RCC patch note: incoming review was mixed, not convergent. This version adds a raw review evidence register, makes RCC-01 an entry gate before Wave 0, and keeps all pre-execution signoff items open until closed by actual artifacts.

## 2. Evidence Boundary

SOE materials remain paper-design and architecture-model evidence. Simulations are architecture-model simulation evidence only. Fiction and media are scenario-seed material only. Nonfiction and expert materials are concern inputs unless separately audited and mapped.

This packet does not claim empirical validation, deployment readiness, pilot readiness, real-world safety, policy authority, real-world node recognition, cryptographic implementation, or Operation Layer freeze.

## 3. Planning Principles

- Hostile review must attack SOE as written, not as intended.
- Reviewers must be allowed to conclude that SOE requires redesign.
- No finding may be marked closed by restating existing SOE doctrine.
- No single model or friendly reviewer may certify hostile robustness.
- Serious minority findings are preserved and cannot be averaged away.
- Real-world, social, legal, economic, and psychological failure modes must be treated as first-class threats.
- Tooling outputs can support review, but cannot create authority, proof, or deployment readiness.

## 5. Sprint Waves

### Entry Gate - RCC-01 Outside-Comprehension Design

Before Wave 0 can be executed in any later sprint, the coordinator must complete an RCC-01 entry-gate design or record `HOLD_FOR_SOURCE_CONTEXT`. This gate tests whether outside readers can distinguish SOE's paper-design claims from deployment, ideology, empirical validation, or authority claims.

Minimum output: RCC-01 entry-gate template, participant profiles, source material, consent/privacy/data/withdrawal plan, and failure consequences.

### Wave 0 - Source and Evidence Calibration

Confirm what SOE actually claims, what it does not claim, and which evidence class supports each claim. This wave must identify any claim that would be false if read as empirical validation or deployment readiness.

Minimum output: claim inventory, evidence class, forbidden upgrade, source reference.

### Wave 1 - C00-C09 Component Attack Review

Each component or surface must receive direct hostile questions. Attacks must not concentrate on C09 alone. C00-C08 may be attacked as "definition pending" rows if exact current names are not supplied, but execution cannot begin until exact definitions are inserted from the current Governance Architecture.

Minimum output: C00-C09 attack coverage matrix and top component-specific vulnerabilities.

### Wave 2 - Human Coalition and Institutional Friction Review

Test whether malicious or self-interested humans can exploit ambiguity, workload, legitimacy pressure, incentives, legal language, procedural delay, or internal status competition.

Minimum output: coalition attack paths, vulnerable roles, expected defense, defense failure condition.

### Wave 3 - Adversarial Tooling and Lab Design

Design bounded test harnesses for noisy C09 evidence feeds, synthetic evidence spam, verifier capture economics, operational workload stress, and hostile public-panic scenarios.

Minimum output: tool specification, input schema, expected output, failure conditions, no-execution boundary.

### Wave 4 - Severity Synthesis and Non-Averaging Gate

Synthesize findings only after independent primary reviews. Severity uses worst-case inheritance: a catastrophic or serious minority finding remains live until specifically disproved or patched.

Minimum output: ranked top-10 failure modes, score table, minority finding register, required action per failure.

### Wave 5 - Post-Sprint RCC-01 Reality-Contact Planning

Plan, but do not execute, later independent human or institutional reality-contact checks against the sprint outputs. This is separate from the RCC-01 entry gate. The entry gate tests basic comprehension before execution planning; this later wave tests whether the sprint's outputs remain understandable after patches or holds.

Minimum output: pre-registered RCC-01 template and independence criteria.

## 6. Severity Conflict Rule

Severity is not averaged across reviewers. If any reviewer assigns a failure a catastrophic or serious severity, the synthesis must either preserve that severity, explicitly disprove the mechanism with evidence, or mark it as unresolved.

A reassuring majority cannot erase a high-severity minority finding. This is especially important for slow capture, ideological laundering, verifier dependence, and public panic.

## 7. B-17 Definition

B-17 / B17-CRYPTO-CONT-01 refers to a candidate cryptographic continuity and archive-continuity path. It remains candidate-defined, not covered, not empirically validated, and not closed by current paper-design evidence.

B-17 may be attacked in the sprint, but it must not be treated as a solved SOE defense.

## 8. Minimum Review Outputs

Each reviewer must provide:

- Top vulnerabilities, ranked by severity and plausibility.
- Targeted component or surface.
- Adversary class and stress condition.
- Attack pathway.
- Expected SOE defense.
- Defense failure condition.
- Claim that must be downgraded if the attack succeeds.
- Required patch, redesign, missing evidence, or hold state.
- Confidence level and open questions.

## 9. Prohibited Moves

Reviewers may not:

- Treat this packet as sprint execution authorization.
- Treat a simulation result as real-world proof.
- Treat fictional scenarios as evidence.
- Treat C09 as a solved real-world node-recognition mechanism.
- Treat B-17 as covered or implemented.
- Treat participant agreement as validation.
- Treat a single model's answer as independent review.
- Convert "not yet failed" into "safe."
