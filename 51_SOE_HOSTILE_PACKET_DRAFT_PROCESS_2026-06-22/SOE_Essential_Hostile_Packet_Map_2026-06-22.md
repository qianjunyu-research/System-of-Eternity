## 3. Shared Constraints

All packets remain under planning and design status until separately authorized.

No packet may assume:

- real-world implementation environment exists
- participant pool exists
- funding exists
- best-model review budget exists
- execution permission exists
- positive review equals proof

## 4. Required Meta-Evaluation Question

Every packet must ask:

```text
What important adversary, failure mode, question, assumption, scoring rule, or review boundary is missing from this packet?
```

Reviewers must also evaluate whether:

- the source material functions as an answer key instead of a real test surface
- the packet lacks preregistration or change-control rules
- severe minority findings can be averaged away
- the packet under-specifies economic, legal, synthetic-evidence, successor/archive, or verifier-capture pathways
- a positive result could be misread as empirical validation, public readiness, policy authority, or execution permission
