It does not authorize hostile sprint execution, participant contact, recruitment, surveys, interviews, simulation execution, tabletop execution, public release, deployment, pilot work, empirical validation, real-world node recognition, policy authority, or Operation Layer freeze.

## Purpose

The hostile-review lane is meant to produce design packets that can survive review by the strongest available models later, when financial and model-budget readiness exists.

Until then, the correct output is packet design, not execution.

## Standing Rule

Every essential hostile packet must allow reviewers to perform meta-evaluation of the packet itself. Reviewers must be invited to identify missing adversary classes, missing questions, bad assumptions, weak scoring rules, answer-key source material, missing preregistration controls, weak minority-finding preservation, execution drift, self-certification risk, and overclaim risk.

Do not create zip files for this lane unless QianJun explicitly asks for one later.

AI-facing review folders remain as ordinary folders with Markdown files and a hash manifest.
