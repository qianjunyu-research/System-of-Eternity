## 5. Hard Review Questions

1. Does the sprint plan force reviewers to find concrete failure pathways rather than philosophical critique?
2. Does it separate adversary classes from background stress conditions?
3. Does it include human incompetence, fatigue, resource scarcity, and ordinary organizational failure?
4. Does it prevent AI-only agreement from becoming a claim of real-world validation?
5. Does it include a future reality-contact checkpoint with a pre-registered prediction that can fail?
6. Does it preserve all boundaries against deployment readiness, pilot readiness, empirical validation, real-world node recognition, cryptographic readiness, and Operation Layer freeze?
7. Does it require raw returned-review evidence before any synthesis claims multi-AI acceptance?
