# SOE Hostile Review Sprint Planning Packet

Date: 2026-06-19

Status: Planning-only packet for a later adversarial sprint

## 1. Purpose

This packet translates the accepted hostile-review preparation package into a concrete sprint design.

The sprint is intended to attack SOE as if adversaries, institutions, operators, and publics are not cooperative. It should expose whether SOE's paper-design mechanisms fail under capture, corruption, propaganda, legal reinterpretation, resource scarcity, organizational fatigue, public panic, and adversaries who have already read the SOE materials.

This packet does not execute the sprint.

## 2. Core Sprint Question

```text
Can hostile reviewers produce concrete, ranked failure pathways that force SOE to downgrade claims, redesign mechanisms, or admit uncovered adversarial surfaces?
```

If reviewers cannot produce concrete failure pathways, that is not automatically a pass. It may mean the packet failed to force enough attack pressure.

## 3. Planning Principles

1. Attack mechanisms, not reputation.
2. Treat adversaries as adaptive and informed.
3. Treat real organizations as tired, status-driven, overloaded, underfunded, and error-prone.
4. Treat public panic as a stress surface, not as proof of coverage.
5. Treat simulations as architecture-model evidence only.
6. Treat AI review as internal structural critique only.
7. Require failure pathways, not general worry.
8. Allow STOP and REDESIGN outcomes.

## 4. Sprint Waves

| Wave | Name | Purpose | Required output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Boundary and source calibration | Confirm evidence hierarchy, no-overclaim rules, and source context. | Boundary acknowledgement and source-gap list. |
| 1 | Mechanism break review | Attack C00-C09, C09, Operation Layer, claim downgrade, ownership, rollback, and evidence routing. | Minimum 10 concrete failure pathways. |
| 2 | Coalition and human-friction review | Combine adversaries with ordinary organizational failure. | Minimum 5 multi-factor failure chains. |
| 3 | Severity synthesis | Rank failures by severity, plausibility, detectability, and patchability. | Top 10 failure table and top 5 lethal combinations. |
| 4 | Reality-contact planning | Design one future falsifiable checkpoint without executing it. | Pre-registered prediction, failure criteria, and STOP/REDESIGN rule. |

## 5. Adversary Versus Stress Separation

The sprint must separate actors from conditions.

Adversary classes are actors or coalitions trying to bend, exploit, launder, capture, or destroy SOE.

Stress conditions are background pressures that make failure easier even without one intelligent enemy.

A good hostile test combines both:

```text
A11 nation-state strategic competitor + S04 public panic + S07 institutional fatigue + C09 verifier ambiguity.
```

A bad hostile test only says:

```text
AI causes public fear.
```

## 6. Added Sprint-Only Adversary Class

The accepted prep package listed A1-A24. This sprint plan adds one planning-only candidate class for review:

### A25. Insider Successor / Inheritor Drift

Goal: preserve the name, authority, archive, or symbolic ownership of SOE while gradually changing its function after the founder, original reviewers, or early custodians are absent.

Tactics:

- inherit legitimate access but reinterpret obligations;
- merge SOE language into a political, corporate, national, or ideological project;
- treat old cautions as resolved by time;
- use archive control to privilege friendly interpretations;
- claim continuity while changing the decision logic.

Failure mode: SOE is not defeated from outside; it is inherited and domesticated into something terminal, captured, or status-preserving.

## 8. Compact Component Attack Index

The current hostile-review prep package does not reproduce the full C00-C08 stack. Therefore this sprint plan must not invent a false complete index.

Before sprint execution, reviewers must receive either:

1. the current Governance Architecture component index; or
2. a compact C00-C09 attack index extracted from the current source documents.

Until then, the minimum attack surfaces are:

| Surface | Attack focus |
|---|---|
| Meta-Governance | Evidence hierarchy, source authority, claim downgrade, null-result handling, non-autonomy rules. |
| Governance Architecture C00-C08 | Baseline continuity, routing, evidence, adjudication, feedback, control, and stability logic as defined in the source architecture. |
| C09 | Node recognition, anti-capture, verifier independence, rollback readiness, and real-world recognition boundary. |
| Operation Layer | Event routing, ownership, custody, successor access, rollback, handoff, appeal, terms-of-engagement, queue overload, and execution authority leakage. |
| Public communication layer | Claim laundering, public panic mapping, misread evidence classes, status confusion, and ideology capture. |

## 9. Minimum Sprint Outputs

A valid sprint return must include:

- ranked top 10 failure pathways;
- top 5 dangerous multi-adversary combinations;
- top 5 plausible human-friction failures;
- at least 3 public-panic mappings that remain bounded as concern surfaces only;
- at least 3 ways AI or synthetic evidence can poison SOE review;
- at least 3 ways SOE can be ideologically laundered;
- at least 2 routes by which an insider successor can preserve name continuity while altering function;
- claim downgrades required by each serious failure;
- whether the failure is partial, serious, or catastrophic;
- whether the right next action is PATCH, SIMULATE, TABLETOP, REALITY-CONTACT, HOLD, or REDESIGN.

## 10. Failure Severity Scale

| Severity | Meaning | Required consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Partial | SOE notices the problem but too late, too vaguely, or without enough authority to act. | Patch or more targeted test. |
| Serious | SOE notices only after material capture, legitimacy damage, or procedural paralysis. | Downgrade affected claims and redesign mechanism. |
| Catastrophic | SOE mechanisms amplify the adversary or convert attack into apparent authorization. | Stop claim, redesign structure, do not proceed to operational framing. |

## 11. Anti-Self-Certification Rule

A sprint cannot pass merely because several AIs agree.

A sprint can only be called internally accepted if:

- returned reviews are attached or cited;
- unresolved warnings are preserved;
- at least one STOP/REDESIGN path remains possible;
- a future reality-contact checkpoint is designed before any hostile-review completion claim;
- evidence boundaries remain intact.
