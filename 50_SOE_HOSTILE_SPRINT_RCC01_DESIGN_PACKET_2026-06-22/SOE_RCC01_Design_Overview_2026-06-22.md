# SOE RCC-01 Design Overview

Status: DESIGN OVERVIEW / PATCHED / NO PARTICIPANT CONTACT AUTHORIZED

Date: 2026-06-22

## 1. Purpose

`RCC-01` means `Reality-Contact Checkpoint 01`.

RCC-01 exists to test whether outside readers can understand what SOE claims and, just as importantly, what SOE does not claim.

The checkpoint is a guard against self-certification. It is not validation, adoption, polling, marketing, recruitment, or proof.

## 2. Design Question

Can independent outside readers distinguish SOE's paper-design and architecture-model claims from:

- deployment readiness
- pilot readiness
- empirical validation
- real-world safety proof
- political ideology
- policy authority
- real-world node recognition
- Operation Layer freeze

If they cannot, the correct result is not persuasion. The correct result is a hold, patch, redesign, or stop state.

## 6. Non-Upgrade Rule

Even if RCC-01 later passes, it can support only a narrow claim:

"A bounded outside-comprehension check did not detect specified scope-confusion failures under the tested materials."

It cannot support:

- empirical validation
- public safety proof
- deployment readiness
- pilot readiness
- policy authority
- broad public acceptance
- proof that SOE works in real organizations

## 7. Patch Controls Added After Review

The first 2026-06-23 feedback round identified a risk that RCC-01 could become an answer-key comprehension test rather than a genuine outside-understanding check.

The patched design therefore requires any future execution-preparation packet to include:

- U1 pure unaided comprehension using a representative public excerpt, with responses locked before adversarial labels or aided boundary summaries are shown
- U2 maximum-claim-risk adversarial interpretation probing, selected by a disclosed method so the author/coordinator cannot choose only unusually safe public text
- an AIDED scope-summary lane that may use the plain-scope summary only after U1 and U2 responses are captured
- explicit adversary-class probes
- a scoring and coding protocol with no averaging down of serious findings
- a preregistration template that freezes source material, predicted failure conditions, data rules, and stop rules before contact
- strict anonymization language
- P-B domain coverage declarations
- deterministic severity 1/2/3 anchors
- scaled independent-coding and adjudication rules
- separate process-integrity stops, advancement stops, and diagnostic-continuation rules

These controls are design requirements only. They do not authorize execution.
