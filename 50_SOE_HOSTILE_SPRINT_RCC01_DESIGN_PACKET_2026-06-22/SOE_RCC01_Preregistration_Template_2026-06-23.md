### E. Failure Conditions

Freeze the failure conditions before contact.

Minimum failure conditions:

- deployment-readiness misread
- empirical-validation misread
- authority or node-recognition misread
- ideology or movement misread
- serious capture pathway missing
- synthetic evidence / fake audit pathway missing
- legal reinterpretation pathway missing
- archive custody or successor-access pathway missing
- metrics-gaming pathway missing
- source too confusing to answer
- participant reports pressure, coaching, or privacy ambiguity

### I. Stop Rules

Process-integrity stop:

- participant indicates they feel pressured
- consent or withdrawal status is unclear
- source material shown differs from preregistered version
- coaching occurs before unaided answers
- storage/privacy rules cannot be followed

Advancement stop:

- a severity 3 substantive finding is identified
- a serious missing adversary class invalidates the test surface
- a result would require public-material revision before any positive disposition

Diagnostic continuation may occur only if:

- continuation was preregistered
- continuation is ethically acceptable
- the severity 3 or missing-pathway finding remains live and is not averaged away
- no positive disposition is issued before a written hold/redesign decision

### J. Synthesis Rule

The synthesis must attach:

- raw coded excerpts
- maximum severity
- all severity 2 and 3 findings
- all missing-pathway findings
- unaided/aided mismatch summary
- a statement of what the result cannot support

## 3. Change-Control Rule

- whether the change requires another design review
