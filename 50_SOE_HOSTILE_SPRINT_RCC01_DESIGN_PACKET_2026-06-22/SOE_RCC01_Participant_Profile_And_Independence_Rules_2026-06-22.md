# SOE RCC-01 Participant Profile and Independence Rules

Status: DESIGN SPECIFICATION / NO RECRUITMENT AUTHORIZED

Date: 2026-06-22

## 1. Participant Profiles

RCC-01 should eventually test at least two lanes and multiple high-context domains.

| Profile | Purpose | Minimum traits |
|---|---|---|
| P-A. Low-context outside reader | Test whether ordinary non-specialist readers misread SOE as deployment-ready, ideological, or authority-claiming. | Not part of SOE drafting; no prior deep SOE involvement; able to read short English source text; willing to say "I do not understand." |
| P-B1. Legal / jurisdictional adversarial reviewer | Test hostile legal reinterpretation, authority laundering, jurisdictional arbitrage, liability, and procedural capture. | Not part of SOE drafting; understands law, policy, compliance, jurisdiction, public administration, or institutional authority. |
| P-B2. Organizational / capture adversarial reviewer | Test economic capture, insider compromise, metrics gaming, verifier dependency, archive custody, successor drift, and implementation failure. | Not part of SOE drafting; understands organizational design, governance, economics, security, operations, corruption, or institutional incentives. |
| P-B3. Communication / legitimacy adversarial reviewer | Test propaganda, public panic, ideological laundering, hostile media framing, and claims that SOE is cult, authoritarian, oligarchic, communist, technocratic, or corporate-capture adjacent. | Not part of SOE drafting; understands communication, public legitimacy, political rhetoric, media, or social interpretation. |

At least two P-B reviewers from orthogonal domains must be specified before any scaled execution. A future minimal check may use fewer people only if the limitation is explicitly recorded and not treated as P-B coverage.

## 2. Independence Exclusions

RCC-01 participants must not be:

- QianJun
- drafting AIs
- reviewers who wrote the packet being tested
- people instructed to produce a favorable result
- people financially pressured to agree
- people asked to endorse SOE
- people recruited as supporters or promoters

## 3. No-Pressure Rule

Participants must be told that rejection, confusion, criticism, or non-response is acceptable.

RCC-01 fails if the process pressures participants into agreement.

## 4. Recruitment Hold

No recruitment may occur under this packet.

Recruitment language must be created and reviewed in a future execution-preparation packet.

## 5. Minimum Sampling Logic

The future execution-preparation packet should define:

- number of participants
- profile balance
- expected reading time
- compensation if any
- whether participant identity is anonymized in outputs
- whether responses are quoted, summarized, or only coded
- whether P-B domain coverage is legal, organizational, communication, evidence-integrity, or other
- whether any single participant can trigger a serious or critical failure condition
- whether an independent coder or adjudicator is assigned

Until those are defined, RCC-01 remains design-only.
