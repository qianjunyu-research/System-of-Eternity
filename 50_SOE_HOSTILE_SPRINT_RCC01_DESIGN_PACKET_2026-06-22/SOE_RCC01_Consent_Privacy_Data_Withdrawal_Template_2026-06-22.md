# SOE RCC-01 Consent, Privacy, Data, and Withdrawal Template

Status: TEMPLATE DRAFT / PATCHED / NOT APPROVED FOR USE

Date: 2026-06-22

## 1. Purpose

This template defines the minimum participant-protection language that any future RCC-01 execution packet must include or improve.

It is not a final consent form.

## 2. Candidate Contact Statement

You are being asked to review a short research-framework summary and answer questions about how you interpret it.

This is not a request for endorsement, agreement, promotion, or participation in a political or institutional project.

You may reject the premise, say the text is confusing, decline to answer any question, or stop at any time.

## 3. Privacy Statement

Unless separately agreed in writing:

- your name will not be published
- your raw response will not be publicly posted
- responses may be summarized as anonymous findings
- identifying details must be removed from internal summaries unless the participant separately gives explicit written permission for attribution
- public quotations are prohibited unless separately agreed in writing

## 4. Data-Minimization Statement

The future RCC-01 execution packet should collect only:

- participant profile type
- date of response
- response text or coded response
- consent/withdrawal status
- whether the response triggered a failure condition

It should not collect unnecessary personal data.

The future execution packet must also state any reasonably foreseeable discomfort or risk, including the possibility that a participant may feel awkward criticizing the author's work.

## 5. Eligibility and Ethics Review

Any future execution packet must define participant eligibility before contact.

At minimum, RCC-01 should not recruit minors. If a future execution design proposes including minors, it must be treated as a separate ethics and legal review problem and cannot proceed under this template.

The execution-preparation packet must also record whether an ethics, institutional-review, legal, or independent privacy review is required for the planned participant contact.

## 6. Withdrawal / Stop Rule

Participants can stop or withdraw before final analysis.

The execution packet must define:

- how to withdraw
- whether withdrawal deletes raw response text
- how long raw responses are retained
- who can access raw responses
- contact information for questions and withdrawal
- what happens to coded summaries after withdrawal
- the deadline after which withdrawal can no longer remove already-anonymized aggregate results, if such a deadline is used

## 7. Retention, Deletion, and Breach Handling

The future execution packet must define:

- retention duration for raw responses
- retention duration for coded/anonymized summaries
- deletion mechanics for raw responses after withdrawal or retention expiry
- backup or archive handling
- who is responsible for deletion
- what counts as a privacy breach
- how participants will be notified if identifying information is exposed

## 8. Compensation / No-Coercion Rule

If compensation is used later, it must not be contingent on agreement, favorable interpretation, completion of every question, or endorsement.

## 9. Current Boundary

This template is not permission to contact anyone. It is a design artifact only.
