6. Cross-Cutting Simulation Constraints
f_min = 0.30
Disturbance coupling floor. Locked by simulation. Prevents full loop collapse. Theoretical derivation absent — stated as empirically validated constraint.
Star topology — MANDATORY AVOIDANCE
Star topology produces 43.3% sustained ignition regardless of hub protection or ceiling reduction. Three-round confirmed v15/v16/v17. No deployment on star topology.
Hub dual role — confirmed
Hub serves as both failure broadcaster AND disturbance absorber. Protecting the hub redistributes load to peripherals. Multi-hub minimum: S_hub = min(all hub nodes).
Federation threshold — UNRECALIBRATED
Federation S_threshold at the ring/mesh value produces elevated false alarms: v18 Sim 2 measured 35.8% for minimum-node aggregation and 25.9% for two-tier aggregation. Do not deploy on federation topology with automatic Trigger A until v19 recalibration completes.
Asynchronous T(t) updates
T(t) updates are asynchronous across nodes. Update timing is topology-specific (C08). No synchronous global T update assumed anywhere in the architecture.
Composite I(t) = w_role·I_role + w_belong·I_belong
This formula applies to Class H nodes only. Class I nodes use I_belong_proxy. Class A nodes use I_role only. Mixed networks require node class declaration at deployment.

These assumptions were identified during multi-AI review. They must be stated explicitly in any arXiv paper. They are not gaps — they are architectural choices that require disclosure.
Assumption | Statement
Continuous trust proxy | T(t) is modeled as a continuous variable. Real institutional trust may be discrete or ordinal. Behavior at discretization boundaries is unstated.
G(t) existence / effectiveness separability | G(t) conflates governance existence with governance effectiveness. A government that exists but is captured still has positive G(t) in current formulation.
SSL timescales asserted | I_role operates on weeks-months timescale; I_belong on months-years. These timescales are asserted as plausible, not derived from identity dynamics or empirical data.
R_base independent activation pathway | When C06 G(t) → 0 (governance collapse), R_base must act alone. The condition defining 'alone' and the pathway is unspecified in C02.
Apply across all components. Locked by simulation v13–v18. No component spec may override.
