Fix 7 — Federation Trigger A Architectural Finding
7.1 Why Trigger A Does Not Fire on Federation
The two-tier S_network aggregation method (lower quartile per cluster, inter-cluster minimum) is designed to absorb local node failures before they propagate to the network-level S_effective signal. This is why federation is safe — local stress stays contained within clusters. The same property prevents S_network from accumulating enough signal to breach S_threshold, even under high disturbance.
The architecture that makes federation safe is structurally identical to the architecture that prevents S-based stress detection. This is not a bug — it is a correct consequence of the two-tier design.
