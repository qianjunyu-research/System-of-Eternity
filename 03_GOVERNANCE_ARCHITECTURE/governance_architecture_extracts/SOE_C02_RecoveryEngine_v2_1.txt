1.1 The Loop — Confirmed
The loop operates as follows: when T falls, dG/dt = γT − δG produces declining G(t). As G(t) falls, k_D_effective rises (less interference suppression from C06 Path 1). Higher k_D_effective allows more disturbance through the network, which drives T lower, which drives G lower further. This is a positive feedback loop — each cycle amplifies the others.
The loop has two natural brakes: R_base's upward pressure on T, and the f_min = 0.30 floor on k_D_effective which prevents full coupling collapse. The question is whether these are sufficient to guarantee loop termination under all disturbance conditions.
