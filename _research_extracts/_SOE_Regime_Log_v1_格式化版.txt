6. Key Finding
In high-control conditions, system behavior is dominated by control mechanisms, and internal state variables become non-binding.
7. Interpretation
The system enters a control-dominated regime where stabilization is achieved regardless of internal structural details.
This masks the role of trust, cognition, and memory, making it impossible to determine their necessity under these conditions.
8. Implications
High stability does not imply correct model structure
Component necessity cannot be inferred from high-control regimes
Additional testing under constrained conditions is required
